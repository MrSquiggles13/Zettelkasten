# Electrical Components

## Notes

There are many different types of electrical components that help make a circuit useful and work.

![[Pasted image 20260406031939.png|148]]
Breadboards are a tool used to prototype and test circuits before they are soldered onto a circuit board.

![[Pasted image 20260406031713.png|155]]
LEDs are diodes that have resistance which emits light. They have an anode (+) and a cathode (-) which only allows current to flow in one direction.

![[Pasted image 20260406032333.png|170]]
Microcontrollers are a type of [[Central Processing Unit]] that can be programmed to carry out a set of instructions in a circuit. They are not very powerful but have a lot of functionality and are used in a lot of electronics. 

![[Pasted image 20260515014636.png|212]]
Ultrasonic Distance Sensors use sound waves to calculate how far an object is from the sensor. A wave is sent out and received then utilizing the speed of sound calculates the distance which is outputted as voltage.

![[Pasted image 20260515015155.png|246]]
DC motors convert electrical energy into mechanical energy by using electromagnetic pulses to spin a metal cylinder. DC motors typically have 2 terminals where the spin direction can be controlled depending on which terminal is grounded with the other fed voltage. Can be housed in gearboxes to increase torque while sacrificing angular speed.

![[Pasted image 20260515020549.png|278]]
The L293d Motor Driver chip controls a DC motor through an H bridge that can flip the polarity of the motor's input. This chip can be fed a higher voltage power source to provide more voltage to the DC motor.

![[Pasted image 20260515021022.png|286]]
A Servo Motor is a type of DC motor that can rotate to precise angles with high torque. Servo motors take in an encoded signal that is processed to determine what angle the motor's gear head will align to.

![[Pasted image 20260515020855.png|266]]
A Photoresistor uses resistance and its photoconductive cells to determine the strength of a light source. Photoresistors are wired in series with another resistor which outputs a voltage reading across it that is proportional to the amount of light shone on the Photoresistor.
