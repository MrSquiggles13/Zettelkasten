# Entrepreneurial Mindset and Technical Skillset

## Notes

Don't Make JUNK!!

The core principles behind an Entrepreneurial Mindset is that when an Engineer is designing and implementing solutions the end user should be at the forefront of their mind. If a solution is disliked or not wanted by the target user then it is not a good solution even if it technically meet all requirements. Also it should be evaluated if the problem at hand even IS a problem. If it ain't broke don't fix it if people are happy with how they are handling it and any improvement would be superfluous then there is no need to make a solution. Just because something can be made does not mean it should be. The technical prowess of an engineer is a valuable skill but how and when to apply it is the key to a great engineer. Great engineers combine the Entrepreneurial Mindset WITH their Technical Skillset.

There are 3 C's to the Entrepreneurial Mindset
- Curiosity - Remaining inquisitive towards the problem at hand will not just get the right answers but help ask the right questions. Consistently researching contemplating and exploring the problem will help iterate over ideas that are potential solutions
- Connections - Being able to tie ideas together from seemingly unrelated subjects is a valuable skill to cultivate. Connecting the right ideas could shed new light and help build insight towards a more innovative solution. Being able to see outside the box and draw new conclusions will keep ideas fresh and interesting so as to make traction for better solutions.
- Creating Value - The main goal of a great engineer is providing a positive influence on their surroundings. The point of building solutions is in the end to help others solve their problems. Without having value for their work engineering as a whole derives no value itself.

When an engineer successfully combines the EM with the TS they are able to reach a certain level of competency that derive desirable traits for others. These include being able to recognize opportunity, apply solutions that meet specific needs, communicate effectively, draw conclusions from data, and synthesize data from multiple sources. When Engineers harness the power of having an EM their value as an Engineer grows exponentially as their technical skills become for surgical to the right problems.
