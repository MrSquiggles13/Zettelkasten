# Engineering

## Notes

Engineering is a discipline that incorporates many facets of knowledge and wisdom to understand how anything works so as to design and create creative solutions to a multitude of problems. Engineers can have a wide range of specialties but should be avid problem solvers and do well in a collaborative setting.

"Even if a topic seems irrelevant to one's discipline it is still important to remain interdisciplinary curious"

Modern day engineering is more improving upon than inventing.
