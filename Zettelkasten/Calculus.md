# Calculus

## Notes

The study of the instantaneous rate of change of a function and its relation to the area under the curve of the function.

Optimizing a problem involves finding a function's minimum or maximum value over a domain. This is done by determining the constraints of the problem, creating an algebraic representation of the objective function, finding the critical numbers of the function and then determining the extreme values in the domain.
