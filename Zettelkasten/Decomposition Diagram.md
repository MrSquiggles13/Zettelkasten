# Decomposition Diagram

## Notes

Decomposition Diagrams come in 3 common forms and involve breaking down a problem into smaller parts or components. Each component can then be broken down even further into smaller parts until it is a fundamental level which can then be solved recursively to feed into the solution of the whole.

There is also the black box method with the same idea yet the focus is on the inputs and its corresponding outputs

These can then be categorized by attribute and designs can be implemented around said attribute. A table can then be made to weigh each design within its attribute row for the best fit and combination with other attribute designs in subsequent rows. This is called a concept combination table.
