# Molecular Models

## Notes

Molecular models help represent different compounds and elements

Molecular formulas use the element names and a prefix to number each unit of element in a compound

Structural formulas use relative placements and connections in a molecule with dot pairs or lines representing the bonds between atoms

Ball and stick models visibly represent the atoms (balls) and bonds (sticks) while also accurately representing the angles between bonds.

Space filling models are accurately scaled up images of molecules and show the relative size of atoms, the distance between their nuclei and the angle of the bonds but these bonds are not directly shown as the atoms overlap.
